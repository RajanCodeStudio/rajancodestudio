Privacy Policy

calendar_today Last Updated: July 30, 2026
info Overview

Only 5G Tools ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles network status monitoring and hidden 5G network settings access. We are committed to transparency and ensuring your personal data remains strictly safe.

shield Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal data.

check_circle No personal identifiers (Name, Email, Phone Number) are requested or stored.
check_circle No network traffic logs or browsing history are monitored or sent to remote servers.
check_circle All network status calculations happen 100% locally on your Android device.
lock Permissions Explained

Our application requests specific system permissions strictly required for core operational functions:

wifi
Network & Wi-Fi Access

INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Used to detect real-time internet connectivity, Wi-Fi SSID, and active network types (4G / 5G / Wi-Fi).

signal_cellular_alt
Cellular & SIM State

READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE Required to read mobile signal strength levels (5-bar indicator) and carrier names (e.g., Jio 5G, Airtel 5G).

location_on
Location Access

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Android system architecture mandates location permission to read cell tower information and Wi-Fi network names. We NEVER record or track your physical GPS location.

extension Third-Party Services

Our application does not integrate third-party data tracking, user profiling, or intrusive advertising SDKs. Your operational usage remains completely private to your local device session.
